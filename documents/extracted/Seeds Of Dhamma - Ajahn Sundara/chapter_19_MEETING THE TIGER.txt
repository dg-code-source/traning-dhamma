## MEETING THE TIGER

All of us are working with strong desire energy and habits. At some point each of us has to meet the roaring tiger within. Don’t think monastics are just sitting peacefully on a cushion in a blissed out state. We are dealing with the tiger day and night, the tiger that is always saying ‘No, no, this is not good enough, I need more, I need more, I need more!’ But in our practice we learn to befriend the tiger.
