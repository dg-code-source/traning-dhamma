## BACK TO THE FOUR NOBLE TRUTHS

We can get lost when we follow the Buddha’s teaching because there are many overlapping lists and terms that are difficult to translate. In order to keep things clear and simple, Ajahn Sumedho would always bring us back to the Four Noble Truths as the central teaching.

The First Noble Truth, dukkha, suffering – this is to be recognized. The Second Noble Truth, samudaya, the origin of suffering, craving based on ignorance – this craving is to be let go of. The Third Noble Truth, nirodha, the cessation of suffering – this is to be realized. The Fourth Noble Truth, magga, the path of practice leading to the end of all suffering – this is to be developed.
