## Appendix 1

#### Thirty-two Parts of the Body

Atthi imasamim kāye In this body there are:

kesā Hair of the head

lomā Hair of the body

nakkhā Nails

dantā Teeth

taco Skin

maṃsaṃ Flesh

nahārū Sinews

aṭṭhī Bones

aṭṭhimiñjaṃ Bone marrow

vakkaṃ Kidneys

hadayaṃ Heart

yakanaṃ Liver

kilomakaṃ Membranes

pihakaṃ Spleen

papphāsaṃ Lungs

antaṃ Bowels

antaguṇaṃ Entrails

udariyaṃ Undigested food

karīsaṃ Excrement

pittaṃ Bile

semhaṃ Phlegm

pubbo Pus

litaṃ Blood

sedo Sweat

medo Fat

assu Tears

vasā Grease

kheḹo Spittle

siṇghānikā Mucus

lasikā Oil of the joints

muttaṃ Urine

matthaluṇgan’ti Brain

Evam-ayaṃ me kāyo uddhaṃ This then which is my body,

Pādatalā adho kesamatthakā, from the soles of the feet up and down from the crown of the head,

tacapariyanto pūro nānappakārassa asucino is a sealed bag of skin filled. with unattractive things.
