## Ninety days in the Whole XII

The turning earth obscures the sun, night comes over England. Vixens bark, badgers trundle out, mother calls the children in.

A breath of sleep and then a skyful of stars as dawn comes. Wake! Again!! Begin!!!

Hollow-legged, blinking; emergence from oblivion and the strange dream-logic wherein vague feelings and half-remembered characters balloon into huge reality then fade without a murmur.

Owl-calls echo through the woods; dew drips, clattering softly on chestnut leaves. Pale violet, rose, the sky fills with light, amethystine.

Venus and the crescent moon have given up their sparkle to the dawn.

Colour and birdsong wash through the hills, the dark is over.

Chithurst, August 1988
