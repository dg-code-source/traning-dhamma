About the Translator 
Santikaro trained as a bhikkhu under Ajahn Buddhadāsa during the 
final years of his life (1985-1993). During that time, Santikaro 
translated for Ajahn Buddhadāsa bot h orally during talks and 
interviews, as well as in printed works. Santikaro continues 
translation and other Dhamma work at Kevala Retreat in 
Wisconsin, USA, and is a close collaborator with the Buddhadāsa 
Indapañño Archives.
