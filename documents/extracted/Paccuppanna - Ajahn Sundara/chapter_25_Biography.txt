## Biography

Ajahn Sundara was born in France in 1946 and studied dance there and in England. In her early thirties, whilst living and studying in England, she attend a talk and later a retreat led by Ajahn Sumedho. His teachings and experiences of the monastic way of life in the Forest Tradition resonated deeply.

In 1979 she asked to join the monastic community at Chithurst Monastery in England as one of the first four women novices. In 1983 she was given the Going Forth as a Siladhara (10-precept nun) by Ajahn Sumedho. After five years at Chithurst, she went to live at Amaravati Monastery, where she participated in the establishment of the nuns’ community.

Between 1995 until 1998 she deepened her practice mostly in Thai Forest monasteries. In 2000, after spending a year as the senior incumbent of the nuns community at the Devon Vihara, she was based at Abhayagiri Monastery in the United States. She currently lives at Amaravati Monastery where she explores ways of practising, sustaining and integrating Buddhist teachings in Western culture. Since the late 1980’s she has taught and led meditation retreats worldwide.
