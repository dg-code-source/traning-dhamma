[Māra:]

“That of which they say, ‘It’s mine,’

And those who speak in terms of ‘mine’ -– If your mind exists among these,

You won’t escape me, ascetic.”

[The Blessed One:]

“That which they speak of is not mine,

I’m not one of those who speak [of mine].

You should know thus, O Evil One:

Even my path you will not see.”

Then Māra the Evil One, realizing, “The Blessed One knows me, the Fortunate One knows me,” sad and disappointed, disappeared

right there.

~ S 4.19, (Bhikkhu Bodhi trans.)
