Pali Phonetics

23

Examples:

th as t in ‘tongue’. It is never pronounced as in English ‘the’.

ph as p in ‘palate’. It is never pronounced as in English ‘photo’.

ḍ, ḍh, ḷ, ṇ, ṭ, ṭh

These retroflex consonants have no English equivalent. They are sounded by curling the tip of the tongue back against the palate.
