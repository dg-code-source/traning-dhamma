# The Key to Liberation

Our life is like a lump of ice. When it is put in the sun, it begins to melt. All conditioned things are like this: arising, persisting for a while, and disappearing.

When you sit in meditation, do not try to become anything. Just watch the natural movement of the breath. If pleasant feelings arise, know they are impermanent. If unpleasant feelings arise, know they are impermanent.
