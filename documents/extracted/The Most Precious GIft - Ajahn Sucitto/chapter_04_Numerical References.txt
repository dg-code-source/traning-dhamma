## Numerical References

Numerical references to the suttas from the Majjhima Nikāya are taken from the Bhikkhu Ñāṇamoli and Bhikkhu Bodhi translation.

Numerical references to the suttas from the Saṁyutta Nikāya and Aṅguttara Nikāya are taken from the Bhikkhu Bodhi translations.

Numerical references to the suttas from the Dīgha Nikāya are taken from the Maurice Walshe translation.
