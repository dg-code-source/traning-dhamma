#### Luang Por Sumedho Open Retreat

The following two reflections were offered in the course of a week-long retreat at Amaravati Monastery (12-19 May 2017), during which Luang Por Sumedho and several senior monastics were teaching. There were up to 400 people sitting in the Temple, including 80 monastics. Over 100 people were camping in the field and about 60 were staying in small port-a-cabins.
