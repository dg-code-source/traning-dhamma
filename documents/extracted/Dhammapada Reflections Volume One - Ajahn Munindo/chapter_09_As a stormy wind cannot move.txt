As a stormy wind cannot move a mountain of rock, so one who contemplates the reality of the body, who develops faith and energy, is unmoved by Mara.

v. 8

Mara is the manifestation of unenlightenment; the driving force of denial. Mara is the avoidance of reality and is expressed as compulsiveness, insensitivity, resentment. Our monastery in rural Northumberland is sometimes buffeted by winds that can be frightening but they are nothing compared to the threat posed by the forces of Mara. If we are to withstand the onslaught of our heedless habits, we must firmly establish our contemplation in an awareness of the body, in faith and in energy. It is the ability and willingness to come back, over and over again, to our body-based practice, remembering to live in ways that lead to selfless confidence that sustains our interest in discovering truth. This has the power to make us unshakable.
