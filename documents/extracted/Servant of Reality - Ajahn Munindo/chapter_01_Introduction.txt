# Introduction

Servant of Reality is a companion volume to Alert To The Needs Of The Journey (Aruno Publications 2018). Each chapter of this small book stands alone, but readers might also find benefit from reading them in the sequence in which they are presented.

This compilation is offered not necessarily to those looking for instruction on Buddhism, but by way of encouragement to anyone interested in deepening their personal contemplations. Rather than giving answers it aims to highlight helpful questions.

The themes offered here for contemplation are the same as those contained in the talks regularly given in the meditation hall at Aruna Ratanagiri Buddhist Monastery. Audio files of related Dhamma talks can be found at:

https://ratanagiri.org.uk/teachings

Once again, many friends and supporters have assisted in preparing this material for publication. I am very grateful to them all.

Ajahn Munindo Aruna Ratanagiri Monastery, Northumberland, Great Britain August 2018
