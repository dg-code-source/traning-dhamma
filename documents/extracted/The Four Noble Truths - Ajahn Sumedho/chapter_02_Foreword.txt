## Foreword

This book, The Four Noble Truths (one of my earliest publications), is based on personal experience investigating the teachings of the Lord Buddha established in his First Sermon 2560 years ago. It has been the focus of my practice and teaching that has meant most during my life as a  Buddhist monk.

In 1979, when we were moving from London to West Sussex to establish Chithurst monastery, Ajahn Sucitto decided to make a calligraphic copy of the Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta for me to use when we chanted. He started with calligraphic presentations of the Pāli teaching, with designs from his imagination, which became more colourful and artistic as he developed them.

We've decided to combine the two in this book – my text of The Four Noble Truths and the calligraphic and artistic interpretations of the scripture. We trust that this will increase your faith and understanding of the Lord Buddha's teaching.

The images themselves are worth contemplating and investigating as they are about the ways of developing sati sampajañña, intuitive awareness, helping to free the mind from delusion and deceit.

I hope this book will be appreciated and read by many.

Ajahn Sumedho
