Boundaries of Time

All this skilful effort pays off by giving you more time to calm and clarify your mind. Make a regular time each day to cultivate some of the preceding exercises and others that you may learn from alternative sources. Set up a good place where you won’t be disturbed for ten minutes or more, shift into embodied awareness and let it process the events of the day, resting in the here and now.

Establishing such a time boundary and resolve will keep you fit, enhance your sense of well-being, and enable you to start the rest of your life on a surer footing.
