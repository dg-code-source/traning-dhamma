To strengthen this overall awareness when you’re at home or in a quiet place, practise widening the boundaries of your attention while remaining centred. Start by sitting, walking or standing with imaginary hoops around your body. Your awareness is then wide but centred rather than drawn out by sights and sounds. It’s like having cat’s whiskers; you’ll feel wide but contained and alert. You’ll sense when your body contracts (a thought or a memory can have that effect) – then Pause at the point where you sense the contraction or agitation and Ask: ‘Where am I now? Where is this in my body?’ As your centredness gathers itself, you can widen and Ask ‘What’s really important for me, now?’ and ‘What helps me to be with this?’ As this ‘gate’ gets established, the appropriate healthy boundary will also establish itself.
