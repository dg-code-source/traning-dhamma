From there you can look into consumer hunger, gossip and absorption into media. How much we shop and what we take in and talk about has its effects. One way to check this out is to Pause as you’re about to engage and Ask: ‘How am I now? What is helpful?’ You can also Pause and Ask after the actions: ‘How does it feel, now?’ This helps you to find your own standards. It’s good to learn what’s suitable for you rather than to simply follow the crowd or adopt a theory.
