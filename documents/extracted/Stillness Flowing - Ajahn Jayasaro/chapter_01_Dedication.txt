# Dedication

Bhavaggupādāya avīci heṭṭhato Etthantare sattā kāyūpapannā Rūpī arūpī ca Asaññī saññino Dukkhā pamuccantu Phusantu nibbutiṁ.

From the highest existence down to the lowest, whatever groups of beings there exist, whether with or without a physical form, conscious or non-conscious, may they all be free from dukkha, and enjoy the happiness of Nibbāna.
