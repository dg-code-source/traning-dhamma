## My Life as a Novice

Piyasīlo Sāmanera (Todd) May 2005 at Abhayagiri Monastery

My life as a novice is fun but hard. The hard part is that you are supposed to wake up early at 4:30 a.m., the fun part is in working to pick up nails with a big magnet. I also like playing with lizards.

The monks are great. We help each other work on dishwashing. They taught me how to wash my clothes, to set up the mats, to get dressed in a robe, and to wash my bowl differently. The monks are fun to be around because they are really nice people. We tell each other stories.

The almsround was interesting but the bowl was heavy for carrying up the hill. Tan Ahimsako was fun to go with though.

I would like to thank Luang Por Pasanno for ordaining me. I would like to thank all the monks for helping me and for being kind to me while I learn to be a novice. I will remember everyone at the monastery always.
