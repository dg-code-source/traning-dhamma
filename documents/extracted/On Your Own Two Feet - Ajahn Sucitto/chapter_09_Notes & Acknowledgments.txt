# Notes & Acknowledgments

It’s time to sit down and express thanks. The original material that this book was based upon was transcribed from various retreats by upasikā Jotakā. The book was written at Cittaviveka Monastery and designed and typeset by Nicholas Halliday. The text, flaws and all, is my own responsibility.

You can obtain copies of this book from various ‘Forest Sangha’ monasteries (see forestsangha.org), of which Cittaviveka (cittaviveka.org) is one. These sites will also provide you with information about visiting the monasteries, and the resources that they freely offer.
