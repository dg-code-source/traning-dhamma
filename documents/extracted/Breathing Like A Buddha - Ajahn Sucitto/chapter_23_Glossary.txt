# Glossary

Here is a list of the Pali words used in this book, along with some of their English translations.

Pali English

ahaṃkāra the ‘I am’ construction

ānāpānasati mindfulness of breathing

anicca changeability, impermanence, inconstancy

anupassi seeing in the presence of, witnessing

anusaya latent tendency, obsession

āruppa formless, immaterial

asaṅkhata the Unconditioned, nibbāna

āsavā outflow, taint, canker, effluent, corruption

atthaṅgamo disappearance

avijjā ignorance

bhava becoming, existence, continuity

bojjhaṅga actor of awakening/enlightenment

byāpāda ill-will, aversion

cetanā intention, inclination, volition, impulse

cetovimutti liberated in heart

citta heart, mind, awareness

cittasaṅkhāra mental formation, heart sensitivity, volitional formation

dhamma/ā quality/qualities, mental object/s, phenomenon/phenomena

dhammavicāya exploration of qualities, investigation of phenomena/states

dukkha suffering, stress, unsatisfactoriness

ekaggatā one-pointedness, unification

ekodhibhāva/ekodhibhūtaṃ singleness, collectedness

jhāna absorption, meditation

kāmacchanda sense-desire

kāma-loka sensual domain

kāya body

kāyasaṅkhāra bodily formation

khandha/ā aggregate/s

manas mind, mentality, intellect

manasikāra attention

nāma name, interpretation

nibbāna untranslated, but rendered as ‘unbinding’ or ‘blowing out’

nibbidā disenchantment

nirodhā cessation, stopping, ceasing

nīvaraṇa hindrance

pajānati directly knows

pāṇa breathing

paññāvimutti liberation in wisdom/discernment

passadhi tranquillity

paṭinissagga relinquishment, abandonment

phassa contact, impression

pīti rapture, refreshment

rūpa form, shape

rūpa-loka fine-material domain

salāyatana six sense-spheres

samādhi concentration, collectedness, unification

samatha calming, stabilizing

sampajāno alert knowing, full awareness, clear comprehension

samudāya arising

saṅkhāra/ā formation/s, fabrication/s, program/s, condition/s

saṅkhata conditioned

saññā perception, felt meaning

sati mindfulness

satipaṭṭhāna establishment of mindfulness

sukha ease, pleasure, happiness

taṇhā craving, thirst

thīna-middha mental stiffness/dullness and lethargy, sloth and torpor

uddhacca-kukkucca restlessness, worry-and-flurry

upadhi attachment, acquisition, residue

upakkilesa refined blemish

upaṭṭhāna establishment (of mindfulness)

vacīsaṅkhāra thought formation, verbal formation

vedanā feeling, feeling tone

vicāra sensing, evaluation, sustained thought, pondering

vicikicchā doubt, scepsis

viññāṇa consciousness

vipāka effect, result (of intended action)

vipassanā insight

virāga dispassion

viriyā energy

vitakka placing, bringing to mind, conceiving

viveka disengagement, withdrawal, seclusion

vossagga release

yoniso manasikāra deep or careful or appropriate attention
