# Welcome to the Book

Before we get involved with the subject matter, I’d like to take a few moments to acknowledge its basis: a basis without which it couldn’t have been written.

During the course of writing this book, and over many years of practice, I have liberally inhaled various translations of the Pali suttas. The versions I draw from are principally those of Vens. Bhikkhu Bodhi and Ñānaponika Mahathera as published by Wisdom Publications of Somerville, MA, 02144, USA (www.wisdomexperience.org). The slightly different translations of Thānissaro Bhikkhu (www.dhammatalks.org) and Sujāto Bhikkhu (www.suttacentral.net) have presented useful nuances to further assist my understanding of how to render the meanings of key Pali terms. Although I have made my own variations in certain areas, I express my gratitude for all their efforts.

This text has been brought into shape through the supportive feedback and editorial skills of Ajahns Gavesako and Karunadhammo, Lisa Gorecki, and the Lotus Volunteer Group.

The typesetting and design have been carefully crafted by Nicholas Halliday.

Anumodana to all concerned!

Ajahn Sucitto Cittaviveka, 2022
