## Abbreviated References to the Pali Canon, with their English Translations

Unless otherwise stipulated, these are all published by Wisdom Publications, Boston, USA.

A: Anguttara Nikāya. Translated as The Numerical Discourses of the Buddha by Bhikkhu Bodhi. Also translated as The Book of the Gradual Sayings by F.L. Woodward, Pali Text Society, Oxford.

D: Dīgha Nikāya. Translated as The Long Discourses of the Buddha by Maurice Walshe.

Dhp: Dhammapada. Translated by Ven. Buddharakkhita (Kandy, Sri Lanka: Buddhist Publication Society, 1985).

M: Majjhīma Nikāya. Translated as The Middle Length Discourses of the Buddha by Nyanamoli Thera and Bhikkhu Bodhi.

S: Samyutta Nikāya. Translated as The Connected Discourses of the Buddha by Bhikkhu Bodhi.

Snp: Sutta-Nipāta. Translated by Ven. Dr. H. Saddhatissa (London: Curzon Press, 1985).

The numbers appended to quotes from these works refer to the specific sutta (or verse), followed by the section within that sutta, followed by the paragraph within that section.
