‘Do not rely upon what you have heard proclaimed, or upon custom, or upon rumour, or upon scripture, or through inference or established principles, or by clever reasoning or favouring a pet theory.

Do not be convinced by someone else’s apparent intelligence, nor out of respect for a teacher.

When you yourself know what is wrong, foolish and unworthy, and what leads to harm and discontent – abandon it.

And when you yourself know what is right – develop it.’
