G L O S S A R Y

Ajahn	(Thai, from Pali Ācariya) teacher, often used as the title of the senior monk or monks at a monastery; also spelt ‘achaan’, ‘acharn’ (and several other ways)

ānāpānasati	the meditative practice of focusing the mind on in- and out-breathing

dependent	a step-by-step presentation of how

origination	suffering arises dependent on ignorance and desire, and ceases with their cessation

dhamma/ā	mental qualities, skilful or unskilful, that are pertinent to the process of awakening

Dhamma	the way it is, the true order of reality; often the Buddha’s teachings

kamma	conscious intended action

khandhā	five ‘heaps’ or categories by which the mind automatically formulates experience. In detail: form (rūpa), feeling (vedanā), perception (saññā), ‘activities’ (saṇkhāra) and consciousness (viññāṇā)

kuti	a secluded and simple dwelling for a monk

or nun
